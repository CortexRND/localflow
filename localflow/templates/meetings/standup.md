---
name: Standup
description: Per-person yesterday / today / blockers for a daily standup.
---
Keep it short: a standup is a status round, not a discussion. Group updates by person when names are mentioned.

## Yesterday
Bulleted list of what each person finished or worked on, as "Name: update" when the speaker is known.

## Today
Bulleted list of what each person plans to work on next.

## Blockers
Bulleted list of blockers or things someone is waiting on, with who can unblock them. Write "None raised." if none.

## Follow-ups
Bulleted list of conversations deferred to after the standup ("take it offline"), with the people involved. Write "None recorded." if none.
